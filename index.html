01 / who

two humans, one long drone

We bring together our individual practices of Dhrupad and electronic sound synthesis. As our moniker suggests, we create long-form ambient listening experiences: a space for one to slow down.

Our sets are listening spaces more than concerts. Sit, lie down, close your eyes and drift. So far we've done it on a barge in Goa, in a 130-year-old Berlin hall, under the trees at Ozora and in a barn in Auroville.

vocalist · pianist · arts manager · researcher

Sahitya

Practises Dhrupad as a disciple of Ustad Wasifuddin Dagar in the Dagarvani tradition. Her practice is grounded in the philosophy of the Dagar gharana, with a focus on alaap: vocal improvisation and the subtlety of vocal sound. She's a Music Therapy graduate and explored the work of sound further through Svaram's Integral Sonic Studies & Practices Program.

@sahityarajagopal ↗

maths teacher · sound artist · accidental musician

Harsh

Creates electronic soundscapes with modular synthesis on VCV Rack and field recordings. He researches and builds installations based on cymatics, and graduated from the Integral Sonic Studies Program at Svaram, Auroville.

Bedtime Sonic Fables

A dream-like journey composed to mirror the fluid transitions of the sleep cycle. It's Dhrupad, electronics and live water-cymatics visuals, often in quadraphonic sound. It started as late-night online sessions in 2024 and was built out with the Serendipity Arts Foundation's Independent Music Production Grant 2025. The debut was in a transformed barn in Auroville, and it toured Europe in summer 2026.

03 / the big one

bedtime sonic fables

dhrupad ╱ electronic soundscapes ╱ cymatics  ·  listen and sleep, listen and dream.

A sonic experience designed to transport you through a dream-like journey, mirroring the fluid transitions of the sleep cycle.

We explore dream states through experimental music and sound, bringing together our practices in Dhrupad, electronic soundscapes and live instruments. It's an ambient performance where you're free to experience it however you wish: sitting, lying down, the possibilities are open.

As an audio-visual set, it comes with an installation of live water-cymatics visuals projected through the room, so you can actually see the sound, through water and light.

stages of a sleep cycle

what the dream is made of

Dhrupad

A form of Indian classical music known for its intricate, expressive structure and meditative quality. The key to our exploration is alaap, which gradually unfolds the emotion and builds up the intensity and expressiveness of a raag. Deep, resonant vocal techniques produce a powerful, earthy sound, carried by drones and live, evolving soundscapes.

electronic sounds
NOTES

Immersive soundscapes with pulsating drones, built with modular synthesis on VCV Rack and field recordings. An hour-long piece that guides you through the phases of the sleep cycle, inspired by the non-linear state of dreams. A conversation between drones and dhrupad vocals, shaped in dialogue with ecology: field recordings of water, fire, air, space and other natural elements.

Cymatics sound made visible

A bass shaker excites a water surface, generating waveforms that evolve in real time with the music, projected onto the wall. Throughout the set we play the visuals too: the shaker's frequency, and the colour and strobing of addressable lights above. It all shifts with the sleep cycle, from deep rhythmic pulses to chaotic, dreamlike distortions.
